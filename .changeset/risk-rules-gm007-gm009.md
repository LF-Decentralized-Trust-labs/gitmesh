Add the third batch of risk rules to the GM table (pivot §8.1 item 3, T1.14). `GM007` (warning) reports an instruction file over the effective-context threshold, citing the ICSE 2026 ETH Zurich context-rot finding (§4.10): it applies to `instructions` and `rule` kinds in every scope (skills/commands/subagents load on demand and stay out), measures raw characters - the only deterministic unit needing no tokenizer - and defaults to 40 000 chars (~10k tokens); §8.1 says "configurable", so the rule is built by `makeGm007(threshold)` for the T1.17 CLI config to construct with a repo-chosen value. `GM008` (info) reports orphan config: an adapter with project- or local-scope artifacts in the inventory but missing from the new caller-supplied `RiskInput.historyAdapters` history evidence (the GM004 lock precedent - honored the day the doctor pipeline computes it; absent field = history unknown = silent, an empty list = explicitly nothing seen; `third-party-managers` exempt per ADR-004). `GM009` (warning) reports inconsistent local-vs-shared hygiene from the new caller-supplied per-artifact VCS flags `tracked`/`ignored` (three-valued; absent = unknown, fires only on explicit evidence): a `local`-scope artifact committed (`tracked: true`), or a `project`-scope artifact gitignored and untracked (`ignored: true, tracked: false`); tracked-but-ignored mixed states and the user/managed tiers stay out, and manager local files are included - the hygiene is the repo's, the remediation a `.gitignore` line, never a write into manager territory. The fixture harness accepts `inventory.json` as `{ historyAdapters, artifacts }` alongside the bare array; five new golden cases run the whole table.
