`gitmesh doctor` respects two more third-party-manager patterns (#504, #505). `.agents/local.json`, the gitignored file `@agents-dev/cli` moves secrets into by design, is inventoried as that tool's `secrets` store and read by no rule, so GM001 no longer reports its values as errors and asks to undo the split. The root files Ruler generates are recognized the way Ruler recognizes them (a first line `<!-- Generated by Ruler -->`, or a `<file>.ruler-generated` sidecar) and labeled `managed by ruler` under every adapter that lists them; GM010 stays silent on a generated `CLAUDE.md`, since an `@AGENTS.md` line added by hand would be overwritten by the next `ruler apply` and the change belongs in `.ruler/`. The Markdown sources under `.ruler/` and `.rulesync/` are no longer walked as nested instruction files by the agent detectors, so `.ruler/AGENTS.md` is listed once, as Ruler's source, instead of once per agent.
