- **Symlink:** one file is a symlink to the other, or both link to one file: zero drift.
- **`@AGENTS.md` import:** the file pulls `AGENTS.md` in with an `@AGENTS.md` line, in `CLAUDE.md` or `GEMINI.md`, whose agents expand imports.
- **Identical copy:** a separate file with the same content.
- **Diverged copy:** a copy that shares some content with `AGENTS.md` and differs in the rest.
- **Pointer:** shares nothing with `AGENTS.md`, and the shorter of the two files, at most three blocks, names the other: a line such as "See AGENTS.md".
- **Separate document:** shares nothing with `AGENTS.md`: a document of its own.
- **Unreadable:** a broken symlink, or content doctor could not read.

34 repositories (7%) have at least one diverged copy. Doctor diffed two or more root instruction documents in 144 repositories (29%) and reported drift in 140 repositories (28%): its drift count includes pointers and separate documents, which it diffs as if they were copies.
